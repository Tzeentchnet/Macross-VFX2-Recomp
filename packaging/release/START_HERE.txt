Macross VF-X2 Recompiled @VERSION@
==================================

Work in progress: the game boots, plays its FMVs, reaches the title and
mission briefing, and enters 3D gameplay. The whole campaign has not been
tested yet.

What you need
-------------
Your own dump of Macross VF-X2 (Japan), serial SLPS-02237: the .cue file with
its three .bin tracks beside it, or a .chd of the whole disc. No game data or
BIOS dump is included; the bundled OpenBIOS is used.

How to play
-----------
1. Unzip this folder anywhere you can write to (not Program Files).
2. Run Macross_VF_X2_Recompiled.exe.
3. Pick your .cue or .chd in the launcher, then press Launch.

You can also start it with your disc directly:
    Macross_VF_X2_Recompiled.exe --disc "C:\path\to\Macross VF-X2 (Japan).cue"

RecompLauncher sets it up for you from recomplauncher.json in this folder.

Where things are kept
---------------------
Everything stays in this folder: settings.toml, memory cards in saves\, and
caches in cache\. To update, unzip a new release over this folder.

English
-------
This release is in Japanese. The fan English patch by NXP1000 and its
translators (https://github.com/NXP1000/Macross-VFX2-English) is not
included, because it has no redistribution license. Building from source
applies it to your own disc; see README.md.
